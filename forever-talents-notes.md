# Warrior Talents — Classic+ ("Forever") beta screenshot transcription

Raw transcription of the talent tooltip screenshot provided 2026-09-12.
This is a rough, overlapping collage of individual tooltip screenshots, not a
clean export — several cards are partially hidden behind others or have
truncated/garbled text where they overlap. Anything I couldn't read with
confidence is marked `[unclear]` with my best guess, or `[cut off]` where the
card is genuinely missing text. **This is a first-pass transcription only —
nothing here has been cross-checked or wired into the sim yet.** Please
correct anything I misread before we build against it.

Compared to vanilla Classic (see `talent-data.js`), this looks like a
significant rework: several talents changed effect/scaling, some moved
between trees (Improved Thunder Clap now under Protection?), a few
Classic talents seem removed (the four weapon Specializations, replaced by
a single "Weaponmaster"), and several new talents appear (Searing Strike,
Boundless Rage, Raging Blows, Precision, Vanguard, Vitality, Focused Rage,
Bastion, Master of Defense).

Screenshot showed "Unspent Talents 29" in the Protection column — so this
build had already spent 22 points somewhere (29 unspent out of 51).
